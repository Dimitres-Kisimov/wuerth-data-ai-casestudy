## Data & figures
- This is an **independent** analysis based on **publicly available information**
  about the Würth Group. It is **not affiliated with, endorsed by, or reviewed
  by Würth**, and uses **no internal, confidential, or proprietary Würth data or
  systems**.
- All Würth-scale figures (company count, employees, revenue, ORSY installations,
  article counts) are **public and approximate**, drawn from general public
  sources, and are labelled as such. No internal Würth figures were invented.
- All performance metrics attributed to my synthetic-data portfolio repositories
  (`revops-optimizer`, `sales-kpi-analytics`, `distributor-intelligence-platform`,
  `agentic-automation-lab`, `agent-flow-studio`, `doc-extract-agent`,
  `automation-roi-explorer`, `route-optimizer`, `bio-efficient-ai`,
  `ml-models-lab`, `logistics-flow-studio`, `logistics-digital-twin`,
  `supply-network-opt`, `market-basket-analysis`, `energy-demand-forecast`,
  `quality-anomaly-vision`, `portfolio-ops`) are measured on **synthetic /
  self-generated data** and are labelled as such throughout. They demonstrate
  method and are not claims about Würth's real business.
- The real-data work uses the public UCI Online Retail II dataset (**CC BY 4.0**,
  1,067,371 raw rows — cited, not redistributed) and every number drawn from it is
  labelled *(real data)*. `retail-analytics-real` is **completed and published**,
  and its honest headline is that the seasonal-naive baseline won its forecast
  comparison. `decision-chain` runs that same real dataset through the whole
  distributor chain and machine-checks 13 reconciliation identities; it
  additionally labels its invented physical layers *(synthetic-assigned)* on every
  ledger line and makes no profit claims.
- `chain-mcp` is the agentic-integration layer (an MCP server): five of its tools
  run on the deterministic synthetic seeded datasets and one (`forecast_demand`)
  runs on the real UCI pipeline, each carrying a per-tool honesty label.
  `quantum-explainer` is an education / prototyping PWA and carries **no**
  business-data metrics — its assertions test the simulator's physics and
  structure, not any dataset.
- Standards named in `logistics-flow-studio` (ISO 22400, ASR A1.8, DIN 15185,
  EN 15512, EPAL/DIN EN 13698, VDI 2510/3564, DGUV) are referenced as design
  guidance and framed "aligned to, **not** a certification".
