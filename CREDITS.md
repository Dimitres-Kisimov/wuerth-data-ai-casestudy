- This is an **independent** analysis based on **publicly available information**

- All performance metrics attributed to my portfolio repositories
  (`revops-optimizer`, `sales-kpi-analytics`, `distributor-intelligence-platform`,

  on **synthetic / self-generated data** and are labelled as such throughout.

  Online Retail II dataset (**CC BY 4.0**, 1,067,371 raw rows — cited, not
